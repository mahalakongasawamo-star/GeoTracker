What changes from the shipped card

  • Chrome: macOS traffic lights + URL bar removed. Two ink hairline rules and a kraft-shift paper field replace the rounded-corner shadow card.
  • Folio: serial-number "01" in Instrument Serif italic sits as a magazine cornerstone, anchoring the page without competing with the headline weight.
  • Verdict: moved from a small coral-deep label below the centered score into an italic Instrument Serif deck paragraph above it. The verdict now reads as an editorial pull-quote instead of decorating the number.
  • Sign-off CTA: the "Try your own →" link becomes italic serif at 18px, matching the deck voice instead of bold coral sans.
  • Score block: hairline rules above and below mark it as a stat insert in a longer story, rather than a hero KPI.

What is preserved

  • Palette: cream, ink, coral, amber/green/red status pips. Coral count drops naturally to 2 (folio dot pulse + verdict word + sign-off).
  • Type system: Geist body, Geist Mono labels, Instrument Serif italic accents.
  • Data fixture: same business, same score, same queries, same grid.
  • Brand voice: editorial authority, calm alarm. The deck does the alarm work the italic "invisible" word does on the landing.
